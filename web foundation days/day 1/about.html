About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for writing down ideas, reminders and tasks the moment they come to mind. It keeps your notes in one place so you can find them again and never lose a good thought.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the "New note" box on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it.
  3. Read your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.
  4. Come back any time to add more notes.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Note counter that shows how many notes you have
  • Simple layout that works on phones and computers

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Ctrl + S Save your work
